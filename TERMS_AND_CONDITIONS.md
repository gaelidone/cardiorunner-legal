Subway Cardio is an interactive physical exercise gamification application. It transforms cardiovascular exercises (such as jumps, squats, high knees, and side steps) into real-time gameplay controls using computer vision and camera-based motion detection, optionally pairing with external display screens (such as Smart TVs, monitors, or PC browsers) via local wireless networks.
