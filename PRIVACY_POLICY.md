## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Subway Cardio** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting your personal privacy and providing complete transparency regarding how data is handled when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Application" or "Service").

Subway Cardio is built upon the foundational principle of **Privacy by Design**. We do not harvest, monetize, or sell your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what permissions our app requires, how motion detection operates, and how we safeguard your privacy.

By installing, accessing, or using Subway Cardio, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the Application.

## 2. Camera Access & Motion Detection (Crucial Privacy Notice)

Subway Cardio utilizes your device's camera to deliver interactive physical exercise gameplay (such as tracking jumps, squats, and lateral movements).

- **100% On-Device Real-Time Processing:** All image frames and camera streams are processed strictly and exclusively in volatile memory (RAM) directly on your local device hardware using on-device computer vision models.
- **Zero Video or Photo Recording:** The Application **never** records, saves, captures, or creates permanent video clips, photographs, or raw biometric templates from your camera feed.
- **No Cloud Uploads:** No camera frames or visual sensor data are ever transmitted to external servers, cloud storage, third parties, or developer endpoints.
- **Immediate Resource Release:** Camera processing occurs only during active workout sessions. As soon as a workout ends or you leave the active training screen, the camera hardware is immediately released.

---

## 3. Local Network & Wireless Connectivity

Subway Cardio offers features that allow your mobile phone to act as an interactive controller paired with a large screen (such as a Smart TV, monitor, or PC browser):

- **Local Communication / WebSocket Relay:** When using screen-pairing functionality, the Application communicates with the display screen via local network protocols (LAN / Wi-Fi) or ephemeral WebSocket rooms using a temporary connection code.
- **Command Signals Only:** The data transmitted over this connection consists exclusively of basic, non-identifying game command signals (such as `JUMP`, `DUCK`, `LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `START`, `PAUSE`).
- **No Private Traffic Inspection:** We do not monitor, record, or inspect any other network traffic or activity on your local network.

---

## 4. Fitness, Workout & Gameplay Data

- **Local Storage:** Any performance metrics generated during your workouts (e.g., jump repetitions, estimated calories burned, workout duration, player score, experience points, or virtual streaks) are stored locally in the Application's private sandbox storage on your device.
- **No Mandatory Account Creation:** You are not required to create an account with an email address or personal identity to enjoy core gameplay.

## 5. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The Application is distributed through official app distribution platforms (such as the Google Play Store and Apple App Store). These platform providers may collect anonymous, aggregated technical metrics in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

- **Operating System Telemetry:** Operating system version, device model, hardware specifications, and non-identifying crash logs to ensure app stability and performance.
- **No Advertising Trackers:** Subway Cardio does not integrate third-party targeted advertising SDKs, behavioral cross-site trackers, or data broker networks.

## 7. Data Retention & Security

- **Data Retention:** Because we do not store personal information on remote servers, your gameplay data exists only on your device. You can erase all locally stored workout history and preferences at any time by clearing the Application's data/cache in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application.
- **Security:** We apply industry-standard security safeguards. Any network communication utilized for screen pairing or remote relay uses encrypted transmission protocols (such as TLS/WSS) to prevent unauthorized interception.

## 8. User Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Regional Laws)

Depending on your geographic location, you may have specific privacy rights regarding your personal information under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

- **Right to Access & Portability:** You may access any locally stored data directly through the Application interface.
- **Right to Deletion:** You may delete all application data instantly by uninstalling the app or clearing local app storage.
- **Right to Revoke Permissions:** You may revoke camera, local network, or notification permissions at any time via your device's operating system settings.
- **Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Info:** We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for monetary or other consideration.
